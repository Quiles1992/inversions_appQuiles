# Signal Lifecycle

## Purpose

Describe the lifecycle of signals and how they are represented in the system.

## Signal Model

- `id`: UUID
- `accountId`: UUID
- `symbol`: string
- `action`: string (`BUY`, `SELL`, `HOLD`)
- `confidence`: number (0.0 - 1.0)
- `sourceCores`: string[]
- `rationale`: string
- `createdAt`: datetime
- `expiresAt`: datetime

## Lifecycle Rules

- Signals are generated by the signal engine and must be explainable.
- Signals may only be considered valid when they include source core convergence and confidence metrics.
- Signals should be stored persistently for auditability.
- Signals may expire after a configured validity window.

## Relationship to Orders

- A `Signal` may be linked to one `Order`.
- Orders created from signals must preserve the original signal rationale and confidence.

## Traceability

- Every generated signal must include a rationale describing the reason for the recommendation.
- Signal records must be append-only to preserve the audit trail.
